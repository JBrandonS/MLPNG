Simulated non-Gaussian temperature and polarization CMB maps


The non-Gaussian simulations have been generated using the algorithm
described in Elsner and Wandelt (2009), ApJS 184 264-270, up to a
multipole moment of l_max = 1024.
The transfer functions were computed with CAMB version February 2009
and RECFAST 1.5.


Simulation parameters:

We used the WMAP5+BAO+SN cosmological data provided by LAMBDA:

Omega_L 	  0.721
Omega_C h^2 	  0.1143
Omega_B h^2 	  0.02256
h 	          0.701
n_s 	          0.96
tau 	          0.084
scalar amplitude  2.457e-9


Description:

Files are stored in FITS format. Each simulation consists of three
files. The spherical harmonic coefficients are provided in
dimensionless units, i. e. dT/T.

You may want to use the FITS2ALMS routine of the HEALPix software
package to load the Gaussian (alm_l_XXXX_v3.fits) and the associated
non-Gaussian (alm_nl_XXXX_v3.fits) part of the simulations.

To generate a CMB map containing non-Gaussianity of local type,
compute alm = alm_l + f_NL * alm_nl, where f_NL can be any desired
level of non-Gaussianity.

Finally, we also provide the power spectrum in dimensionless units
(cl_wmap5_bao_sn.dat), containing, in this order, l, C_l^TT, C_l^EE,
and C_l^TE.
